# Code of conduct

BRO Backup is made by people who back up for each other. Treat each other the same way here.

## What we expect

- Be friendly and patient, also with people who are new to NAS devices, Docker or backups.
- Assume good intent. Say what you think of an idea or of code, not of the person.
- Accept that a maintainer says no to a feature. It is not a judgement of you.
- Keep other people's data private. Do not post someone else's addresses, tickets, logs or screenshots
  without their consent.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, harassment, threats, or discriminatory jokes or remarks.
- Sexual language or images.
- Publishing private information of others.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after a clear no.

## What happens

Maintainers remove comments, issues or pull requests that break this code, and can block people from the
repository, temporarily or for good. They decide case by case and do not have to explain each decision in
public.

## Reporting

Use **Report content** in the menu of a comment, issue or pull request. Depending on the repository
settings it goes to the maintainers or to GitHub Support. Do not report security problems this way: see
`SECURITY.md`.

This code applies to everything in this repository: issues, pull requests, discussions and commit messages.

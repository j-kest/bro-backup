Space for bros

Tip: put the bro-backup folder on its own dataset with a quota and daily ZFS snapshots. That protects your bros against ransomware, even without append-only.

Local network

For bros on the same network, or on VLANs that can reach each other. They then connect directly instead of via a relay. Up to 4 addresses, separated by a comma. The app must also publish UDP ports 7841 and 7842 on the same numbers: add "7841:7841/udp" and "7842:7842/udp" under ports. Your bros get the new address right after you save.

Updates
